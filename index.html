Questions people ask

Is hotel pick-up included?

Yes, for all tours in Dubai, Sharjah and Ajman. Abu Dhabi and Ras Al Khaimah pick-ups may carry a small extra charge, which we tell you before you confirm.

How do I pay?

Send your request on WhatsApp. We confirm the price and share payment options. Nothing is charged on this website.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes. Changes and cancellations are free up to 24 hours before pick-up. After that, the tour operator's policy applies.

Do you pick up from the airport?

Yes. We track your flight, so a late arrival does not cost you extra waiting time. Terminals 1, 2 and 3 at DXB, plus DWC and Abu Dhabi airports.

Are tours suitable for children and families?

Yes. Most tours welcome children. Tell us their ages so we can arrange child seats and the right activities.

What should I wear on a desert safari?

Light, comfortable clothes and closed shoes. Bring a light layer for the evening, as the desert cools after sunset.
